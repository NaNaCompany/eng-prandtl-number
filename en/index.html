FORMULA

Pr = μc_p/k

Assumptions

Required: μ,c_p,k>0 for the same fluid state. μ is dynamic viscosity, not kinematic viscosity. Effective non-Newtonian properties are not estimated.

Displayed values are floating-point approximations. Design safety factors and unprovided error contributions are not calculated.
